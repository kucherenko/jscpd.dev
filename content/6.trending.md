# Trending Repos, Analyzed

How much duplicated code ships in GitHub's trending repositories? Every day a [GitHub Actions pipeline](https://github.com/kucherenko/jscpd.dev/blob/master/.github/workflows/trending.yml) picks the day's trending repos, runs **jscpd v5** on each, and publishes the results here.
